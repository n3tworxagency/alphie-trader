ABOUT ALPHIE TRADER

One app.
Every tool you need.

Alphie Trader is a mobile trading application that brings together live market data, copy trading, portfolio management, a social community, and a practice account in one unified experience.

The platform is designed for users at all levels, from those who are new to financial markets and want to explore in a practice environment, to more experienced participants who want access to market data, community, and portfolio tools on the go.

Alphie Trader is regulated by the Seychelles Financial Services Authority (FSA) and is currently available in early access. Register now to secure your place ahead of the full launch.
